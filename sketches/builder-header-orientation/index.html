Concept B · reference

Journey line — breadcrumb and stepper become one track

The insight: the breadcrumb and the stage stepper describe the same journey, so draw them as one line. Past stops sit left of the you-are-here dot (clickable, they go back). Future stops sit right (dashed track = not yet reachable). The lock reason hangs directly under the locked stop. Tools sit in a bordered cluster off the track.

Concept C · reference

Two decks — place on dark, progress on light

Deck 1 (dark): identity and navigation only — back link, title, version, tools at far right. Deck 2 (light): a full-width plain-English progress sentence plus a real progress bar that doubles as the stage control; "Send to workers" is a button at the end of the bar, disabled with its reason shown in the sentence, not a padlock. The stepper numbers ("1 Edit → 2 Send") disappear entirely.
