Concept B · abandons the metaphor

Library tree + "Who can see this?" panel

No lines at all. Left: the library as folders — collections open to show their SOPs, exactly like a file explorer everyone already knows. Right: a panel that always answers one question about whatever is selected: "Who can see this?" Departments are checkboxes with people counts; a green summary sentence states the total. Selecting a SOP explains that it follows its collection, with a one-click jump to change it at the source.

Concept C · middle ground

Access cards — one card per collection, sentence on the face

The library as a grid of collection cards. Each card face answers the question before it's asked: "Seen by Forming and General — 23 people." "Show SOPs" expands the card to list what's inside; "Change who sees these" flips the card into a department checklist in place. Browsing and permissioning are the same surface, and there is no diagram to learn.
