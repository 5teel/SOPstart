Concept C · reference

Access cards — one card per collection, sentence on the face

The library as a grid of collection cards. Each card face answers the question before it's asked: "Seen by Forming and General — 23 people." "Show SOPs" expands the card to list what's inside; "Change who sees these" flips the card into a department checklist in place. Browsing and permissioning are the same surface, and there is no diagram to learn.
