# Sketch 013: People, made visual

## Design question
People (admin and safety manager) answers "who is on site, where do they sit, and what are they allowed to do?". Today: department cards with text tags (Signs off N / Assigns / Approves), a ✓/— table, and no training or supervision picture.

## How to view
Open `.planning/sketches/013-people-visual/index.html`. On B, click Chloe Park or Mike Rolleston (the supervisor icon) to trace who they look after.

## Variants
- **A: Cards with icons**: today's department cards with faces, authority icons (grey when absent), a training bar per person, and a sheet with "what they can do" sentences plus a training snapshot linking to Training.
- **B: Org chart**: sketch 001's winning node chart, built from data that exists today: site → departments → roles with filled-of-planned counts and dashed empty seats. Clicking a supervisor draws lines to their people and fades everyone else.
- **C: Who can do what**: the authority table as an icon grid grouped by department, with how many people hold each power and a training column. Gaps such as "Nobody here signs off" show on the group row.

## What to look for
- Are the six icons learnable, or do they need their labels on every row?
- B is the most visual but the widest: does it hold up at 15 departments (see sketch 003 for the scaling answer on wiring)?
- "Nobody here signs off" is information about a department, not a nag. Does it read that way?
