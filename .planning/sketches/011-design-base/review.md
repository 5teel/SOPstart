# SOPstart design review: the impeccable lens

_Method: single context. The skill's detector, live-server and browser-overlay steps were skipped as the brief asked. Evidence comes from 20 screenshots in `.planning/evals/latest` (63-home-list-area, -map-site, -read-worker, -phone-list, -phone-read, -phone-map, -phone-signoffs, -section-record, -section-training, -sections-admin, 58-walk-hazard, -walk-ppe, -walk-photo-required, -phone-walk, -edit-admin, -browse-real-org, 59-inbox, -inbox-empty, -people, -signoff-open) and from the source. Rules come from `SKILL.md`, `reference/critique.md`, `craft-floor.md`, `operate.md`, `mode-operate.md`, `layout.md`, `typeset.md` and `colorize.md`._

## 1. Lens

Impeccable judges whether a screen was authored for this product or assembled from category defaults. Its "product slop test" asks whether the screen could ship unchanged in an unrelated product. On Operate surfaces (app UI) it wants "earned familiarity": standard controls, colour that does real jobs, and a world that contributes only type, palette, density and one named signature move. It has an explicit Refuse list: eyebrows, coloured side-borders, nested cards, template empty states, badges standing in for content, and one identical entrance on every page.

## 2. Verdict

The structure is sound, but the surface is the stock Tailwind/shadcn kit. The neutrals are zinc, the accents are Tailwind's 500/600 hues used as-is, and the app repeats a set of habits: tinted uppercase pills, 4 px coloured left bars, uppercase eyebrow labels over every group, an icon-in-a-circle empty state, and a violet sparkle "AI" box. Two pieces are clearly authored: the hazard-tape wordmark and the isometric site map. They prove the product has its own world, but nothing else on screen carries it, so the app reads like a competent template wrapped around two good ideas.

## 4. Recommendations (ranked by impact ÷ effort)

1. **Remove every coloured left bar.** Delete `KIND_EDGE` (`focus/KindChip.tsx:11-16`) and the `border-l-4` in `WalkStep.tsx:122`, `BrowseDocument.tsx:126`, `admin/StepCard.tsx:213` and `FocusRail.tsx:75`; mark the current rail item with `bg-paper-1 font-semibold` alone. In `SopRow.tsx:39`, drop the `w-1` bar and put the area colour as a `size-2 rounded-full` dot at the start of the meta line, which the group header already does. The hazard and PPE callouts become one 1 px `border-accent-hazard` frame plus the tint fill, with a solid `bg-accent-hazard text-paper` signal-word strip across the top. Effort **S**. No ADR conflict.
2. **Separate the area palette from the semantic accents, and remove the duplicate blue.** Redefine `--area-1..8` in `blueprint-theme.css:200-207` as a muted, lower-chroma OKLCH set (for example `oklch(0.62 0.07 h)` across eight hues) so no area shares a hex value with an accent. Merge `--accent-measure` and `--accent-step` into one blue and render CHECK as an ink-outlined chip (`border border-ink-300 text-ink-700`). Effort **S**. Enforces ADR-0004 rule 8 rather than conflicting with it.
3. **Keep red for safety only.** In `office/InboxRow.tsx`, delete the severity dot (`:269`) and map the `stuck` and `owner` entries in `CHIP_STYLE` to ink (`border border-ink-300 text-ink-700`). Use amber `--accent-decision` only for overdue items, and leave red to HAZARD and failures that block a worker. Effort **S**. Supports ADR-0004 rule 8.
4. **Drop pills that carry no information.** On `PeopleTab.tsx:52`, show no status chip for ACTIVE and show "Invited" only as the exception. On the read list (`ReadView.tsx`) and in browse mode, show `KindChip` only for hazard and ppe; ordinary steps need no STEP tag. Effort **S**. No conflict.
5. **Replace the AI-check styling.** Remove `Sparkles` and the `--tint-ai-*` frame (`AiCheckBanner.tsx:139-147`) and render it as a plain section: a `text-reading font-semibold` heading such as "2 things to check", then findings as divided rows (`divide-y divide-ink-200`), not cards inside a card. Retire `--ai` and `--tint-ai-*` from `blueprint-theme.css:132,161-162`. Effort **S**. No conflict.
6. **Remove the eyebrows.** Delete "WHAT YOU'LL DO" (`ReadView.tsx:182`) because the numbered list needs no caption. Delete "BEFORE YOU START" in `WalkStep.tsx:114` because the HAZARD/PPE signal word already says it. Turn `SopList.tsx:24` `LABEL` into real headings (`text-reading font-semibold text-ink-900`, sentence case: "Recent", "Most used", "All SOPs"). Keep caps only for safety signal words (HAZARD, PPE), which are an ISO 3864 / ANSI Z535 convention, not decoration. Effort **M** (about 15 call sites). **Conflicts with ADR-0006**, which says "labels and tags are caps, weight 700, +0.06em." The ADR would need to narrow to "tags and signal words are caps; section labels are sentence-case headings."
7. **Use one segmented control and one chip size.** Pick the List/Site map control as the single segmented style, extract it, and use it for Inbox tabs (`OfficePane.tsx:102`), Area/Type and Read/Edit. Raise the filter chips in `InboxTab.tsx:60` from `h-9` to `min-h-tap rounded-lg`. Make the Training buttons (`TrainingMatrixView.tsx:268-277`) `min-h-tap rounded-lg px-4 text-ui font-semibold` in sentence case. Effort **M**. Enforces ADR-0004 rule 10.
8. **Make empty states teach.** In `CompletionList.tsx:27-40`, remove the icon circle and show one ghost row in the real row shape ("Your first finished SOP lands here, with who signed it off"), keeping the Find a SOP button. In `InboxTab.tsx:173-180`, replace the CheckCircle and the slogan with a plain ink line such as "Nothing waiting for you", followed by the existing "N cleared today". In Training, name the filter that emptied the result and offer a reset. Effort **S**.
9. **Fix the contrast floor and the browser surfaces.** Change the locked rail items in `FocusRail.tsx:75` from `text-ink-400` to `text-ink-600`, and let the lock icon carry the state. Give the native selects and date inputs the same `min-h-tap rounded-lg border-ink-300 bg-paper-1` treatment and set `accent-color: var(--ink-900)` on `body[data-theme="paper"]` for checkboxes. Effort **S**.
10. **Cut the page explainer lines.** Remove the grey subtitle sentence on Training, Sign-offs and People, or shorten it to a data line ("7 people · 2 supervisors"). Effort **S**. No conflict.
11. **Remove the generic page entrance.** Drop `.page-shell-enter` (`globals.css:18-26`, a 4 px fade-up on every route). `craft-floor.md` warns against "one identical entrance on every section". Effort **S**. Supports ADR-0004 rule 9.
12. **Extend the signature move to replace the bars.** Rows and the read header could carry the map's own isometric glyph for the SOP's object (noticeboard, machine, area plate) in the area colour, instead of a bar or a dot, so the list and the map share one visual language. Effort **M-L**. Fits ADR-0004 rule 5 ("make SOPs visual"). It must not use brand yellow, per rule 8.

## 5. What already works

- **The wordmark is authored.** The SOP ink chip, the lowercase Saira "start" and the hazard-tape foot, merging with the Start button, form a product-specific motion moment with a stated state change (`blueprint-theme.css:762-805`, ADR-0004 rule 9). This is exactly the "one authored moment" `craft-floor.md` asks for.
- **The isometric site map is a real signature move.** It shows one object per SOP, area plates are outlined and clickable, and the phone version has numbered plates with a legend grid (63-home-map-site, 63-home-phone-map). It is the "service health drawn as a transit map" kind of idea that `mode-operate.md` asks a world to contribute.
- **The walk screen passes the focus rule.** It shows one step at a time, step text at 28 px, a full-width black CTA at the bottom in the thumb zone, and verbs in the product's own language ("I'm wearing it — continue", "Add a photo to continue"). It removes everything else (58-phone-walk, 58-walk-photo-required).

## 6. Skill fit

Impeccable's Operate rules fit an industrial safety tool well: earned familiarity, colour with a job, consistent controls, empty states that teach and the contrast floors all hold up. Its "go bold / out-of-distribution" ethos does not suit a safety app and should be ignored here. Its blanket ban on caps labels also collides with safety-signage convention, so apply it to section kickers and keep caps signal words (HAZARD, PPE) as they are.
