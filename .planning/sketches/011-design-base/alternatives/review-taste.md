# SOPstart: design-taste-frontend review

**Design read (skill §0.B):** a preserve-mode redesign of a safety-critical industrial product UI for tradespeople who wear gloves, with a trust-first, signage-like language, leaning toward the existing Tailwind v4 tokens in `blueprint-theme.css`. There is no design-system swap.
**Dials (§1.A trust-first / accessibility-critical):** target VARIANCE 3, MOTION 2, DENSITY 5. Measured on the live app: about 3 / 3 / 5, so layout and density are already right. The slop sits in micro-detail, not in structure.
**Mode (§11.A):** Redesign, preserve. IA, routes, the wordmark and nav labels stay untouched (§11.F).
**Screenshots reviewed (19):** 63-home-list-area, 63-home-map-site, 63-home-read-worker, 63-home-sections-admin, 63-home-section-training, 63-home-phone-list, -read, -map, -signoffs, -record, 58-walk-hazard, 58-walk-ppe, 58-walk-photo-required, 58-phone-walk, 58-edit-admin, 58-browse-real-org, 59-people, 59-signoff-open.
**Pre-flight (§14) on the parts that apply:** FAIL on em-dash, eyebrow count, decorative dots, middle-dot rationing, micro-meta sentences, floating top-right sub-text, progress bars with a filled track, shape consistency (three toggle families) and Button Contrast (disabled primary). PASS on theme lock, serif discipline, CTA wrap, nav on one line, no fake screenshots, reduced motion, and `min-h` with no `h-screen` heroes. N/A: hero, logo wall, bento, marquee.

---

## 1. Lens
This skill values removing the patterns that make a UI look machine-generated. Its rules: zero em-dashes, rationed eyebrows, middle-dots and status dots, no AI-violet, no default Inter-plus-zinc, and every component taken out of its library default state. Every decision must come from a stated design read of the audience, not from habit.

## 2. Verdict
The structure is not generic. One-step-per-screen walking, an isometric map of the real site and a wordmark built from hazard tape are specific to this product. The surface reads as AI-built because of the micro-detail on almost every screen: the 60 px primary buttons carry em-dashes ("I understand — continue"), every block has a caps eyebrow over it, coloured dots sit before every row, and meta lines run on middle-dots. A violet "AI check" banner with a sparkle icon and the stock Tailwind zinc ramp complete the default look. About 70% of the "too Claude" impression can be removed with S-effort string and class edits and no layout work.

## 4. Recommendations (ranked by impact divided by effort)

1. **Remove every em-dash from visible strings.** In `src/lib/sop/focus.ts:120-126`: `'I understand'`, `"I'm wearing it"`, `'Next step'`, `'Confirmed'`, `'Review and send'`. Rewrite the other ~28 strings (list in cue 1) as two sentences or a comma, for example `'Draft. Not published yet.'` and `"Couldn't send it. Your steps and photos are saved. Try again."`. Add `tests/lint/no-em-dash-ui.spec.ts`. It should scan string literals and JSX text under `src/components` and `src/lib/sop`, strip comments and normalise CRLF first (see the 2026-09-28 and 2026-10-08 learnings), and be registered in a playwright project regex. **Effort S.** No ADR conflict.
2. **Take the violet and the sparkle off the AI check.** In `AiCheckBanner.tsx:143-147`, use `bg-paper-1 border-ink-200` instead of the `--tint-ai-*` styles. Replace `Sparkles` with `ListChecks` in `text-ink-700`, title the panel "Wording check", and change the run button to "Check the wording". Delete `--tint-ai-bg/-border` and point `--ai` at `var(--ink-700)` until something needs it (`blueprint-theme.css:132, 161-162`). **Effort S.** No conflict: ADR-0004 rule 8 forbids decorative colour, and violet here is decorative.
3. **Delete the explainer subtitles and the map hint.** Remove the `<p>` at `SignOffsSection.tsx:29-31`, `MyRecordSection.tsx:19` and the matching lines in TrainingSection and PeopleSection. Remove `SiteMap.tsx:204-205`, because the hover lift (`.map-plate:hover`) and the cursor already carry that hint. **Effort S.** No conflict.
4. **Cut the eyebrows to signage only.** Keep caps Saira only on the kind tags (HAZARD, PPE, STEP, CHECK), which really are signs. Make `SopList.tsx:24` LABEL `font-label text-meta font-semibold text-ink-700` in sentence case ("Recent", "Most used", "All SOPs"). Delete the "What you'll do" h2 (`ReadView.tsx:182`), since a numbered list needs no label. In `WalkStep.tsx:114`, render the group eyebrow only for `kind==='step'|'check'`, because on hazard and PPE steps the chip already says it. Drop the `padStart` numbering in `EditRail.tsx:64` and `EditDocument.tsx:67`. **Effort S.** **Conflicts with ADR-0006** ("Labels and tags are caps"). Amend it to "tags are caps; section labels are sentence case".
5. **Replace middle-dot chains with structure.** Render meta as separate inline spans with `gap-x-3` (`ReadView.tsx:127-129`, `SopRow.tsx:43-45`), for example `<span>EVAL Area Packing</span><span>Process</span><span>8 min</span>`. Change "Picks up at step 1 of 1 · or begin from step 1" to "Picks up at step 1." with a separate "Start again from step 1" link. Shorten `SiteMap.tsx:202` to the org name. **Effort S.** No conflict.
6. **Drop the decorative dots.** Remove `SEVERITY_DOT` and the `<span>` at `InboxRow.tsx:269`, since the chip carries severity. In `SopRow.tsx:49` and `ReadView.tsx:138`, drop the dot: "Signed off 9 Oct" reads alone, and where state needs an icon use `Check` for signed and `Pause` for stopped. Remove the area dot at `SopList.tsx:175` and `ReadView.tsx:126` and keep the 4 px area bar on the row as the single area cue. **Effort S.** Consistent with ADR-0004 rule 8, which still keeps one area-colour cue.
7. **Give each meaning its own blue, or stop sharing one.** Point CHECK at `--accent-signoff` (green, "confirmed"), or make CHECK ink-outline only (`KindChip.tsx:8`). Set `--accent-measure` apart from `--accent-step` and `--area-2` in `blueprint-theme.css:103, 119, 201`. **Effort S.** This enforces ADR-0004 rule 8 ("one semantic accent per meaning"), which the current tokens break.
8. **Rewrite the empty states.** Replace `CompletionList.tsx:27-43` with one left-aligned line in `text-reading text-ink-700` ("Nothing finished yet.") and a text link "Find a SOP". Remove the icon circle. Use token utilities instead of `[var(--…)]`. Apply the same to the centred grey "No people with required SOPs in this cut." on the Training screen. **Effort S.**
9. **Recompose the walk screen.** Pick one progress indicator: keep the phone top-bar "Steps · 1 of 5" and the desktop rail, and delete the bar and the "Step N of M" line at `WalkStep.tsx:103-108`. Keep the `aria-live` span. On desktop, remove `flex-1` from the content column (`WalkStep.tsx:101`) so the dock sits right under the step. On phone keep `max-lg:sticky`. Fix the disabled primary at `WalkStep.tsx:206`: `bg-ink-300 text-ink-500` is about 3.3:1, so use `bg-ink-100 text-ink-700` with a `Lock` icon. **Effort M.** No conflict (ADR-0004 rules 6 and 10 still hold).
10. **Unify the selection controls into one `Segmented` component.** Use `rounded-lg`, `min-h-tap`, and active `bg-ink-900 text-paper` (not `text-white`). Use it at `OfficePane.tsx:102`, `SopList.tsx:154`, `HomeShell.tsx:196` and `InboxTab.tsx:60`, so the filter chips become `rounded-lg min-h-tap` instead of `rounded-full h-9`. **Effort M.** This fixes an existing ADR-0004 rule 10 breach.
11. **Treat hazard and PPE cards as signs, not pastel alerts.** The `border-l-4` + `/10` tint recipe in `WalkStep.tsx:18-21` is the default Tailwind alert. Use a solid colour header band with the tag in `text-paper` and the step text on `bg-paper-1`. Consider AS 1319 / ISO 7010 colours (warning yellow-black, mandatory-PPE blue). **Effort M.** **May conflict with ADR-0004 rule 8:** PPE in blue would collide with STEP blue, and yellow is reserved for the wordmark. This is the open "signage colours" decision in `.planning/HANDOFF.md`, so it needs Simon.
12. **Move the neutral ramp off stock zinc.** Keep Inter: the skill's own §4.1 override allows it for accessibility-first products, so it agrees with ADR-0006 here. Change the neutrals in `blueprint-theme.css:93-100, 126-129` to a slightly cool "steel/concrete" ramp, for example `--paper #f3f4f2`, `--paper-1 #fcfcfb`, `--ink-900 #15181b`, `--ink-700 #2c3135`, `--ink-500 #6b7277`, `--ink-300 #cfd3d4`. Re-check AA contrast for `ink-500` text. **Effort M.** **Touches ADR-0004 rule 8** (one design source). That is compliant, since the change happens in that file, but it changes the look across the whole app.

## 5. What already works
- The wordmark (`blueprint-theme.css:761-805`, the SOP chip plus hazard-tape foot) is a real brand asset made for this domain, not a template mark. §11.F says to preserve it.
- The isometric site map is a real visual of the customer's own site (63-home-map-site, 63-home-phone-map), the opposite of a §4.8 div-built fake screenshot. Its motion has a clear purpose (§5 "motion must be motivated") and has a reduced-motion fallback (`blueprint-theme.css:826-828`).
- The walk screen focuses on one step with 28 px text and one 60 px ink CTA (58-walk-photo-required). That hierarchy uses weight and position rather than raw scale (§9.B), and it suits gloves.

## 6. Skill fit
Weak to moderate. The skill states that dashboards, dense product UI and admin panels are out of scope (§13), and most of its rules (hero discipline, image generation, MOTION 6, bento and marquee) assume a marketing page, so they do not apply to a safety tool. It is still useful as a mechanical tell-list for an app that "looks like Claude made it" (em-dash, eyebrow, dot, middle-dot, AI-violet, library-default states). Apply only that part. Safety and legibility rules like ADR-0004, ADR-0006 and signage standards outrank its taste preferences.
