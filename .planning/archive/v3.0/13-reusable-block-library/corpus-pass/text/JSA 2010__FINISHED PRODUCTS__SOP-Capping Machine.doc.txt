
DEPARTMENT |QUALITY CONTROL |TASK |CAPPING MACHINE USE | |
|PURPOSE             |To test bottle breaking point     |SAFETY     |Glasses, gloves & hearing protection       |

|1      |Wearing PPE put the cap on bottle, then the bottle in canister.                                       |
|2      |Place canister in vee of centring jig.                                                                |
|3      |Be aware of Ram moving down. Press and hold start buttons, located either side of the control panel.  |
|4      |Depress start buttons after one Ram cycle.                                                            |
|5      |Remove cap and bottle from canister. If bottle has fractured empty the canister directly into a bin.  |
|6      |Repeat the process for each bottle                                                                    |
|7      |Turn off power when leaving the machine.                                                              |
|8      |In the event of bottle breakage, remove the canister from the capping machine and dispose broken glass|
|       |into the bin provided. Ensure safety glasses are worn when handling broken glass.                     |
|9      |Do not make any adjustment on the machine. In the case of machine failure, notify QC for appropriate  |
|       |action.                                                                                               |
