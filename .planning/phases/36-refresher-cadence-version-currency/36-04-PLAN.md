    - Increment `outdatedCount` / `refresherOverdueCount` on both the row accumulator and the column accumulator alongside the existing `competentCount`/`signedOffCount`/`needsSupportCount` increments. Leave those three counters' logic untouched (D-05: the outdated tally is APPENDED, never subtracted from competency counts).
