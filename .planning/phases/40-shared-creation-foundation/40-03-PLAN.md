<objective>
Make `ParseJobStatus` the one job-progress component, serving both the document/AI parse flow and the video-generation pipeline, with one plain-language stage vocabulary and one realtime-plus-polling implementation.

Purpose: DUP-03 / D-07 / D-08 — today two structurally different implementations of the same idea ship side by side: `ParseJobStatus` (realtime on `parse_jobs` + a flat 5s-delay poll, stage-set keyed by `input_type`) and `PipelineStepper` + `PipelineProgressClient` (realtime across four tables + a grace timer AND a stale watchdog, stage derived from cross-table state). The three-timer model is strictly more robust and is the one that survives; the stage vocabulary becomes one worker-plain sequence mapped over untouched DB keys.

Output: a `src/lib/admin/job-stages.ts` vocabulary module, an extended `ParseJobStatus`, a deleted `PipelineStepper.tsx`, and a `PipelineProgressClient` stripped to its outcome CTAs.
</objective>
