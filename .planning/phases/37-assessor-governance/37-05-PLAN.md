| T-37-05-03 | Information Disclosure | assessment-request list leaking another org's members | mitigate | `listAssessmentRequests` reads with the SESSION client, so `users_see_own_notifications` (`user_id = auth.uid()` AND `organisation_id = current_organisation_id()`) is the enforcing gate; no service-role client is used on this read path |
