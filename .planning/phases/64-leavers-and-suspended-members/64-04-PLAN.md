<objective>
Make "a row exists in organisation_members" stop meaning "is a member" everywhere in the app: every identity check, target check and population goes active-only, records and name labels keep everyone and say "(left)" / "(suspended)", the pickers offer active people, and a guard keeps it that way. This is RESEARCH's key insight ("the dangerous part is the ~30 places that treat a row as membership") done as one sweep under one lint.

Decisions implemented: D-06 (app-layer readers close too; service-role paths cannot trust a non-active row), D-09 (records keep everyone; "(left)" / "(suspended)" where a name is shown in governance views), D-10 groundwork (the active-only member set turns a departed owner's SOPs into the existing unowned Inbox rows), D-11 (pickers offer active people; reassignment through the existing actions), D-04 guard (no membership delete in src/), Claude's discretion: the guard in tests/lint/.

Purpose: MEMB-11 and the guard half of MEMB-06; label half of MEMB-05; SOP half of MEMB-09.
Output: active.ts, statusSuffix, the sweep across ~18 files, member-status lint, matrix sentence; deployed regression run of the Office and requests evals.
</objective>
